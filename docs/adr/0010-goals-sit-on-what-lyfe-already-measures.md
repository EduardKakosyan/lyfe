# Goals sit on what lyfe already measures

The owner wants to set goals beyond the routines: a net worth to reach, a certification to pass, and study time tracked toward them. lyfe already keeps two things apart for every routine, showing up (a streak) and getting better (a progression), and research on goal pursuit points the same way: monitoring what one does moves what one does, monitoring an outcome moves the outcome, and neither does the other's job. So a goal is not a new kind of tracking. It is a destination and a date put on a figure lyfe already reads, and it names the routine that moves it. It has no streak of its own.

Three shapes cover what was asked: a figure to reach, up or down (net worth, a breath hold, the weight trend, the body-fat estimate); a total to build up (hours on a study subject); and a result to get (a certification), which has no percentage, only a countdown and the goals under it. Doing something a number of times a week is not a goal shape, because that is a routine and lyfe has those. A goal is shown against its pace, a straight line from where it started to its figure on its date; behind pace is shown as an amount and what would now be needed, never as a failed state. lyfe may suggest moving a date and never moves one, as with food targets.

Three goals are active at once and the rest are parked. A goal that ends, reached or dropped, is kept with its history, and changing one's figure or date is recorded, not overwritten. The word "goal" means only this; what a day is held to stays a target.

Study comes first as the routine that most goals here lean on (ADR 0002 put it second). It is timed in the app: a session counts once its timer passes 45 minutes, 90 is the target, a day counts once, and four days keep the week with the gym's recovery rule. Build sessions count for at most two a week, and only with an explain-back. Each session is on a subject, and may carry the questions answered and right the first time, because practice results predict exam results where hours studied do not.

## Consequences

- Net worth needs a balance kept for each account each day, since the bridge has no history; a goal on it reads the month's last day, not today's swing, and puts what the owner put away beside it as the part they control. The line starts the day balances start being kept.
- A goal's progress is worked out by the backend from what it already stores, as streaks are. The phone only shows it.
- Weekly study prompts follow the gym's rules: only when the streak is at stake and today is a day to act on it.
- A scored mock interview every four weeks waits until the model on the Spark has been checked against the owner's own marking of about twenty answers.
- The weekly rate of weight change on Progress is not a goal under this wording, and is called a rate.
